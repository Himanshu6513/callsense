How it works

Your team keeps calling. We do the rest.

  1. 1

    Your team calls as usual

    Calls go through your business calling app, such as Superfone or Exotel, as they do today. Team on personal mobiles? We'll help you move to a business calling app with one shared number.

  2. 2

    Each call is analysed

    When a call ends, the recording comes to us. Within minutes it is transcribed, and the mood, key details and next step are pulled out.

  3. 3

    You see it and ask it

    Open the dashboard, get alerts and the weekly report, or ask ChatGPT or Claude about any call, any agent, any week.

Setup takes about a week, done with you on a call. On Superfone your team changes nothing; other calling apps such as Exotel we connect on request. On personal mobiles, we first help you move to a business calling app.

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Which languages does it understand?

Hindi, English and Hinglish work best. We also handle Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi. We test your languages on your own calls during the free trial, before you pay anything.

Which phone systems does it work with?

Superfone works today, with no change for your team. Other business calling systems that save recordings, such as Exotel, MyOperator, Knowlarity and Ozonetel, can usually be connected. Calling from a dialer inside Shiprocket, GoKwik or another panel? If it saves recordings, we can usually pull them in. Tell us yours in the form and we'll confirm on the setup call, before the trial starts. If your team calls from personal mobiles, we'll suggest a simple business calling app.

Superfone already gives me call summaries. What's different?

A summary describes one call. CallSense fills in the same fields from every call, so you see totals and trends across all of them: how many hot leads this week, the top reasons people didn't buy, which agent converts best. You also get alerts and can ask questions across months of calls.

How accurate is it?

On clear calls, transcripts are very close to what was said. Noisy lines and heavy accents can cause mistakes in individual words, but the key details and mood usually come through. Every insight links back to the call and its transcript, so you can listen and check.

Do I need a ChatGPT subscription?

No, the dashboard, alerts and reports work on their own. To ask questions inside ChatGPT or Claude, you need a plan that allows custom connectors, such as ChatGPT Plus or Business, or Claude Pro or Team.

Will my team feel watched?

Most teams find it helpful once they see it: no more call notes to fill, and the best calls become examples for training. We suggest you tell your team it's for coaching and follow-ups, not punishment.

Is it legal to record customer calls?

Yes, businesses in India routinely record customer calls for quality and training. What matters is telling callers, with a short “this call may be recorded” message at the start, and using the recordings only for your business. We set up that message for you and keep the recordings private.

Who is behind CallSense?

CallSense is a small Indian team that builds AI call analysis for businesses that sell on the phone. We're onboarding our first businesses now, so you work directly with the founder, and we set everything up with you rather than leaving you alone with software.

Does it work with my CRM or Excel?

Yes. Call details can be exported to Excel or Google Sheets, or sent to your CRM. For real estate we can match each call to its 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing or Facebook lead; for D2C brands, to the Shopify order and whether it was delivered or returned.

How long does setup take?

About a week. We agree on the fields and labels with you on a call, connect your calling system, and check the first few days of calls with you before you rely on it.

What happens to my data if I stop?

You can take an export of your calls and data, and we delete everything from our systems when you ask.
