FAQ

Which languages?

Hindi, English and Hinglish. Most major Indian languages too, tested on your calls first.

Which phones?

Superfone, Exotel, MyOperator, Knowlarity, Ozonetel. On personal mobiles, we set up one shared business number.

Superfone already gives summaries. What's different?

A summary covers one call. CallSense counts across all calls: who wasn't called back, why people didn't buy, which agent converts.

Is recording calls legal?

Yes, for quality and training. We set up a short "this call may be recorded" message for you.
