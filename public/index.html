FAQ

Which languages?

Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Arabic, Spanish and 50+ more, including calls that switch languages. We test it on your calls first.

Which phone systems?

Superfone, Exotel, MyOperator, Knowlarity, Aircall, RingCentral and most systems that record calls. On personal mobiles, we set up one shared business number.

My phone system already summarizes calls. What's different?

A summary covers one call. CallSense counts across all calls: who wasn't called back, why people didn't buy, who on your team converts best.

Is recording calls legal?

Yes, with notice. We set up the "this call may be recorded" message for you.
