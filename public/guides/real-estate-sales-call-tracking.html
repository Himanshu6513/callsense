Real Estate Sales Calls: What to Track to Get More Site Visits

Updated 20 October 2026 · CallSense

Short answer: For real estate enquiry calls, track the buyer's budget, configuration (such as 2 or 3 BHK), preferred location, timeline, whether they need a home loan, whether a site visit was booked, and the reason if it wasn't. Add a lead temperature and a next step with a date. Captured from every call, these show which leads to chase today, why visits don't happen, and which agents convert.

Why calls are where deals are won or lost

Most property buyers first speak to someone on the phone, from a portal lead, an ad or a hoarding. What gets said on that first call, and how fast the follow-up happens, decides whether a site visit gets booked. Yet most of it never reaches the CRM.

The fields worth capturing from every call

  • Budget (for example ₹70 to 85 lakh)
  • Configuration: 1, 2, 3 BHK, plot, villa
  • Location and any project named
  • Timeline: ready to move, under construction, just exploring
  • Home loan needed or not
  • Site visit: booked, date and time, or not booked
  • Main objection: price, location, possession date, builder trust
  • Lead temperature and next step with a date

Three reports every owner should see weekly

  1. Hot leads not called back within 24 hours.
  2. Reasons visits didn't happen, counted across all calls.
  3. Agent comparison: calls, visits booked, and how often each agent asks about budget and timeline.

How to capture it without extra work

Asking agents to type all this after every call rarely works. Recording calls through a business calling app and analysing them automatically fills these fields from the conversation itself, in Hindi, English or Hinglish, with a link back to the call.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good site-visit conversion rate from enquiry calls?

It varies widely by city, project and lead source. Measure your own baseline from a few weeks of calls, then track it week by week rather than comparing with other businesses.

How quickly should a real estate lead be called back?

As fast as possible, ideally within minutes for new portal and ad leads. Interest drops quickly, and buyers often enquire with several builders at once.

Can channel partner calls be tracked too?

Yes, if their calls go through a business number that records calls, the same fields can be captured and reported per partner.
